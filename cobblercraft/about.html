Our mission

Repair should always beat replace

We believe a well-made shoe is worth three rebuilds. Every job that comes through the door gets assessed by a cobbler, not a counter clerk, and quoted honestly — including the times we tell you it isn't worth fixing.

Our apprentices train for four years before they touch a customer's welt. That is slow, expensive and completely non-negotiable.
